Case Study

Document AI Ops Intake

A document-heavy operations workflow designed to transform mixed maintenance and service records into validated, structured datasets, exception queues, and spreadsheet-ready outputs for downstream business use.

Open dashboard Open summary Back to portfolio
Problem Framing

Document intake is a process problem, not only an extraction problem.

Operational teams often receive requests and updates through scattered files with weak structure and uneven data quality. The real need is to convert those inputs into a controlled workflow that can be reviewed, validated, and reused.

Published Outputs
  • 18 approved records ready for business use
  • 11 queued exceptions isolated for review
  • CSV, XLSX, SQLite, and HTML delivery formats
Workflow

The intake lane registers every file, extracts operational fields, applies normalization, validates the result, and publishes clean records separately from exceptions.

Pipeline Sequence
  1. Generate a synthetic inbox with multiple operational document types.
  2. Register files in an intake layer with metadata and traceability.
  3. Extract fields such as asset, site, priority, category, cost, and routing.
  4. Normalize values into a consistent reporting structure.
  5. Apply validation before publication.
  6. Split approved rows from exceptions.
  7. Publish spreadsheet-ready outputs and a lightweight dashboard.
Why The Control Layer Matters
  • Missing asset references are caught before reporting.
  • Invalid priorities and malformed dates do not pass silently.
  • Negative quantities or costs are isolated for review.
  • Business users still receive outputs in formats they can work with immediately.
Portfolio Relevance

This case supports a stronger Data, AI, and Automation narrative because it shows how semi-structured business inputs can be transformed into validated, repeatable operational outputs instead of remaining trapped in manual review loops.
