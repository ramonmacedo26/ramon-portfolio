Sales visibility built around target attainment, not just charts.

Business Angle

A scorecard that helps teams react before the month closes.

Problem

Commercial teams often know the total result, but not where performance is drifting or where corrective effort should be concentrated.

Dashboard role

The report compares realized sales against plan, surfaces the gap clearly, and makes regional or segment performance easier to discuss.

Portfolio value

It shows practical Power BI communication skills: choosing business KPIs, simplifying the view, and keeping the message executive-friendly.

What This Case Signals

Simple topic, strong presentation, immediate business relevance.

Core KPI

Target attainment

Shows whether the plan is being met and how much recovery is still needed within the period.

Decision Support

Variance visibility

Makes shortfalls visible enough to trigger follow-up by territory, segment, or seller group.
