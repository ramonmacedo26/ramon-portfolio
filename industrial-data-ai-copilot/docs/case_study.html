Case Study

Industrial Data & AI Copilot

A compact predictive maintenance case built to show how raw operational records can be structured, validated, modeled, and translated into business-friendly maintenance decisions.

Open dashboard Back to portfolio

Situation

Maintenance and operations teams often receive sensor logs, work orders, cost events, and inspection reports in separate files. That creates slow prioritization, manual reconciliation, and weak visibility into which assets deserve attention first.

Task

Build a small but realistic data product that ingests raw operational files, validates them, creates analytics-ready tables, and produces clear maintenance recommendations for non-technical users.

Action

  • Generated synthetic industrial data with deliberate quality issues to make validation meaningful.
  • Loaded raw files into Bronze tables without over-modeling too early.
  • Created Silver layers with validation flags and a centralized quality log.
  • Modeled Gold summaries for equipment health, cost exposure, open work orders, and risk scoring.
  • Produced AI-style work-order briefs from operational reports and maintenance context.
  • Rendered a static HTML dashboard aimed at recruiters and hiring managers.

Result

The final output makes it possible to identify high-risk equipment, understand why each asset is risky, and act on open work orders without manually reading every source file.

Stack

Python, SQL, SQLite, HTML/CSS, data quality checks, medallion architecture, operational analytics, and AI-style summarization.
